      A simple project pipeline

      Like a data centre processing information, every step has a clear purpose: plan, build, protect, launch, and keep it running.

      1. 01

        Plan

        Understand your goals, systems, data, risks, and budget. Agree the design and scope.

      2. 02

        Build & process

        Configure cloud, apps, networks, and data processing flows. Automate repeatable setup.

      3. 03

        Secure & validate

        Protect access, validate data and backups, and test that everything works.

      4. 04

        Deploy

        Move approved workloads or data, switch over in stages, and confirm the launch.

      5. 05

        Operate

        Hand over clear instructions, monitor health, resolve issues, and improve.
