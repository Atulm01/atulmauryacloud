Microsoft licensing, made clearer

Compare the model before you choose a plan.

A practical overview of how common Microsoft business products are licensed and billed. We summarize Microsoft’s public guidance in our own words; use the official links for current product rights and terms.

Per-user subscription

Microsoft 365 Business

Compare: Business Basic, Standard, and Premium bundle different combinations of online services, desktop apps, security, and device management. Match the bundle to each user’s actual needs.

Microsoft 365 Business plans ↗

Per-user subscription

Microsoft 365 Enterprise

Compare: Enterprise editions package productivity, identity, security, and compliance capabilities at different levels. Confirm which features are included and which need add-on licenses.

Microsoft 365 Enterprise plans ↗

Usage-based + commitments

Microsoft Azure

Compare: Pay-as-you-go follows eligible resource usage. Reservations suit steady, defined workloads; savings plans trade a set hourly spend commitment for discounts across eligible usage. Commitments have terms and unused value may not roll over.

Azure savings plans and reservations ↗

User, capacity, or consumption

Power Platform

Compare: Standalone user licenses, capacity add-ons, and pay-as-you-go meters cover different app, automation, and data scenarios. Some Microsoft 365 plans include limited rights; those are not a substitute for every premium capability.

Power Platform licensing overview ↗

Product and user based

Dynamics 365

Compare: Licensing depends on the business application and users’ access needs. Base, attach, team-member, and capacity rights can differ; validate each role and scenario against the current product guide.

Dynamics 365 licensing guide ↗

Per-device or per-user subscription

Windows Enterprise

Compare: Enterprise options include per-device licensing and E3 or E5 user subscriptions. Subscription activation generally steps up eligible Windows Pro devices; check the base license and deployment requirements.

Windows licensing details ↗

This comparison is a planning aid, not a license quote or legal interpretation. Microsoft pricing, availability, entitlements, and terms vary by country, agreement, and product changes. Confirm the current Microsoft terms for your exact users and workloads before purchase.
